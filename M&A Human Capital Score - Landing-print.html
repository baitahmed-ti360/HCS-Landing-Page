What it is

A pre-close risk model for M&A decisions.

The M&A Human Capital Score™ does not replace financial, legal, operational, or HR due diligence. It adds a structured read of the human and organizational risks that often sit outside the financial model.

Can this organization actually deliver the value creation plan?

What it assesses

The execution conditions that determine whether the deal thesis can be delivered.

HCS assesses the company being acquired, not the buyer, investment team, or advisory team.

Each dimension is scored. Each finding is tied, where possible, to financial exposure, integration risk, or first 100-day priorities.

Target company · scored on 4 dimensions
01 Leadership & Talent Continuity Will leadership and critical talent hold through the transaction?
02 Culture & Governance Will cultural norms and governance enable fast, aligned, and accountable execution after close?
03 Operating Model & Execution Capacity Is the organization structured and equipped to deliver the value creation plan at scale?
04 Strategic Capability & Future Readiness Does the workforce have the capabilities to deliver the thesis today, and as the business evolves?
The problem

Most deal models assume the organization can execute the plan.

Every acquisition has a gap between what the deal thesis assumes the organization can do and what it can actually deliver. That gap is rarely tested before close.

When those assumptions are not tested, the risk appears later.

What surfaces post-close
Synergies are delayed Months 3–9
Key people leave First 12 months
Leadership capacity is stretched From day 1
Integration slows down Quarters 2–4
Operating discipline weakens Year 1+
Value does not materialize on schedule Year 2 onward

HCS gives deal teams a way to see those risks before they commit capital.
