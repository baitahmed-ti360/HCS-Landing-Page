The problem

Most deal models assume the organization can execute the plan.

Every acquisition has a gap between what the deal thesis assumes the organization can do and what it can actually deliver. That gap is rarely tested before close.

When those assumptions are not tested, the risk appears later.

What surfaces post-close
Synergies are delayed Months 3–9
Key people leave Months 1–12
Leadership capacity is stretched Months 1+
Integration slows down Months 6–12
Operating discipline weakens Year 1+
Value does not materialize on schedule Year 2+

These aren't surprises. They're untested assumptions.

What it is

What the financial model assumes: tested, before close.

The M&A Human Capital Score™ is a pre-close diagnostic that tests whether the target organization can deliver the value creation plan. It sits alongside financial, legal, and operational diligence. Not as a replacement, but as the read those workstreams aren't built to give you.

Findings are scored, tied to the deal thesis, and translated into dollar exposure and Day-1 priorities.

Built for the investment committee. A scored exhibit, not a narrative report.

What it assesses

Four pillars. Eleven dimensions. One score.

HCS scores the target company across four execution-risk pillars and eleven diagnostic dimensions. Every finding rolls up to a composite, then rolls down to dollar exposure or a Day-1 priority.
